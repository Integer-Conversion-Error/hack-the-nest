# Picking events for my food truck this weekend

I own one food truck and I have been invited to six events this weekend. I can't do them all, because some of them clash. I want to pick the set of events that makes me the most money.

Here is what I expect to earn in profit at each event:

| Event | When | Expected profit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farmers market | Saturday 8 am to 12 pm | $420 |
| Street fair | Saturday 10 am to 4 pm | $650 |
| Outdoor concert | Saturday 3 pm to 9 pm | $500 |
| Night market | Saturday 6 pm to 11 pm | $450 |
| Brunch pop-up | Sunday 9 am to 1 pm | $380 |
| Football game | Sunday 12 pm to 6 pm | $700 |

These pairs clash, so I can do at most one event from each pair:

- Farmers market and street fair (the times overlap)
- Street fair and outdoor concert (the times overlap)
- Outdoor concert and night market (the times overlap)
- Brunch pop-up and football game (the times overlap)
- Football game and outdoor concert (the football game needs a full evening of food prep on Saturday)
- Football game and night market (same reason)

Any other combination of events is fine. There is no limit on how many events I attend, as long as no two of them clash.

Please tell me which events to attend and the total profit I should expect.

The football organizers say the crowd might be bigger than usual. I would also like to be able to rerun this with the football game's profit raised from $700 to $900 to see whether my plan changes.
