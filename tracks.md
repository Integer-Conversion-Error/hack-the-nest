# Hack The Nest 2026 Tracks

November 14–15, 2026 at Eastern Michigan University in Ypsilanti, Michigan. The [organizer’s track cards](https://hackthenest.tech/#tracks) and [MLH’s event categories](https://www.mlh.com/events/hack-the-nest-96) are the sources for this guide. The descriptions below are rewritten from the published details.

### Future of Intelligence

OpenAI leads this track with Exa. Create an original AI agent, application, or tool that can reason about information, search the live web, and act on what it finds. Build with OpenAI models and Exa’s AI-oriented search engine. Look for a problem where you wish the tool already existed, then refine the experience to the standard of a usable product.

Each hacker gets $50 in Exa API credits. The organizers say top teams will receive prizes and list the specific awards as forthcoming.

### Mind and Machine

Connect brain signals to software in this brain–computer interface track led by g.tec with PiEEG. You can build a video game, an assistive technology prototype, a data science project, or something in a direction of your own.

- g.tec staff set up and test Unicorn EEG hardware at the event and are available at the g.tec booth.
- g.tec offers a 60-minute hands-on workshop on Saturday at 2 p.m.
- Seven real brain-signal datasets are available. Examples include a P300 speller, steady-state visual evoked potentials (SSVEP), stroke rehabilitation, and hand poses recorded with electrocorticography (ECoG).
- PiEEG provides 16-channel Octopus EEG boards, simulated feeds through PiEEG Cloud, and a live online workshop.

| Placement | Prize |
| --- | --- |
| First | One g.tec Unicorn Hybrid Black headset for the team |
| Second | Two PiEEG Octopus 16 boards |
| Third | One Logitech MX Keys S keyboard per team member |

### Future of Money

Breez presents ten bounties based on everyday situations where moving money is slow or difficult. Pick a bounty and use the corresponding Breez starter template to build a working application with the Breez SDK for Bitcoin and Lightning payments. Write up how you built it so the project can serve as an open-source implementation guide for other developers and their AI coding agents. The Breez team provides help in a dedicated support channel.

**Prize.** Every entry that meets Breez’s criteria earns a bounty. The track card gives no bounty amounts.

### Best Beginner

Enter with a team whose members are mostly first-time hackathon participants. Judges focus on the team’s progress and learning during the event and give less weight to polish.

**First place.** One Logitech MX Master 4 mouse per team member.

### Capture the Flag

This cybersecurity competition runs alongside the hackathon throughout the weekend. Decode ciphers, exploit intentionally vulnerable applications, and inspect hidden files. Finding flags earns leaderboard points. The winner is determined by who finds the most flags.

**First place.** One Flipper Zero per team member.

## On the train

### Beyond the Horizon

The Michigan Space Grant Consortium supports this track using NASA’s open data. Build an analysis, map, model, game, or educational tool. The organizers also welcome Michigan-focused work on subjects such as Great Lakes ice, algal blooms, or heat in Detroit.

- NASA’s APIs offer a single free key for resources such as Astronomy Picture of the Day, Mars rover imagery, near-Earth asteroid information, space weather, wildfire data, and storm data.
- Landsat offers over five decades of Earth imagery, with enough detail to study an individual farm field.
- MODIS covers the planet every one to two days and can be used to study ice, clouds, and wildfire smoke.
- NASA Worldview offers a browser-based globe for exploring imagery.
- NASA’s open Prithvi foundation models cover Earth observation and weather, with versions suited to flood mapping and identifying burned areas.
- Space research resources include ISS experiment data, exoplanet light curves, and observations from Hubble, JWST, and TESS.

The track is supported under NASA grant 80NSSC20M0124.

**First place.** One Celestron Travel Scope 60 telescope per team member.

### Quantum Computing

Quantum Rings lets you simulate quantum circuits on a laptop. Build a game, a visualizer, or an algorithm demonstration, and explain the role of quantum computing in everyday language. Every hacker can get a free 200-qubit Quantum Rings license for one year. Quantum Rings also offers a virtual workshop.

**First place.** A guaranteed interview for the Quantum Rings 2027 summer internship program, compute credits, and a Turtle Beach Stealth 700 headset for each team member.

### Code of Life

Seqera, the team behind Nextflow, provides tools to build or extend a bioinformatics pipeline for research or education. Use public or synthetic data. The track excludes real patient data and medical advice.

- Each team gets a workspace on the Seqera Platform with an AWS compute environment set up for the track.
- Seqera’s AI Co-Scientist can help write, run, and debug pipelines.
- Prebuilt nf-core pipelines come with test data so teams can run an analysis early in the event.
- Twelve public and synthetic datasets cover beginner through advanced projects. Options include single-cell RNA sequencing of immune cells, ClinVar disease-associated variants, synthetic records from Synthea, drug–gene guidelines, TCGA cancer genomics, and DepMap gene dependency screens.
- Teams can extend Eduomics, a community pipeline that simulates data for teaching genomics, to cover other types of biology data such as bioimages.
- Suggested themes include education, precision medicine, single-cell biology, cancer, and health data. Teams can propose their own topic.

**First place.** $250 in Seqera compute and Co-Scientist credits, continued access to the platform, and up to eight hours of mentoring from Seqera bioinformaticians.

### Department of Computer Science Track

Eastern Michigan University’s Department of Computer Science recognizes the strongest software implementation. Projects from any track, idea, or technology stack can qualify. Judges examine the design of the code, the algorithms, and the quality and robustness of the implementation.

**Winning team.** A $1,000 scholarship, listed as $250 per team member.

## Major League Hacking challenges

### Build Your Hackathon A-Team with SpaceXSI

Use Cursor as the primary AI coding agent and integrate a Grok Bot automation into the development workflow. Explain how you used both tools in the project description. Submit the project and opt into the Make it Legendary with SpaceXSI raffle.

**Prize.** A Cursor-branded mechanical keyboard for each member of the team selected in the raffle.

### Best Use of Gemini API

Use Google’s Gemini API to power an AI feature in the project. The published examples include a chatbot that offers personalized advice, a tool that analyzes and summarizes research papers, and an application that generates code, scripts, or music.

**Prize.** MLH swag kits.

### Best Use of Solana

Use the Solana blockchain and take advantage of its fast confirmations and low fees. Examples include a game or social app that needs frequent transactions, a trading or lending application, a decentralized exchange, or a high-volume prototype for supply chains, identity, or payments.

**Prize.** One Unruggable hardware wallet per winning team member.

### Best Use of Tiger Data

Use Tiger Data’s PostgreSQL capabilities for real-time streams, time-series measurements, or analytics. The platform supports querying large datasets with SQL, storing relational records and high-frequency metrics together, updating dashboards from precomputed continuous aggregates, and compressing large volumes of events. Example applications include IoT monitoring, AI analytics dashboards, and financial forecasting. MLH’s listing emphasizes innovation, impact, and performance.

**Prize.** Stream Deck Mini.

### Best Use of Vultr

Use Vultr cloud services to deploy or power a project. Options include one-click deployments, scalable cloud compute, and GPU infrastructure for AI workloads. MLH’s listing invites participants to sign up for free cloud credits.

**Prize.** Assorted prizes. MLH gives no itemized list on the event page.

### DEV Writing Challenge

Publish an article on DEV about the project and share what other developers can learn from how it was built. DEV editors pick at least one favorite each month and may pick more depending on submission volume. The selection factors are technical substance, originality, clarity of storytelling, and community popularity.

**Prize.** $100.

### Best Use of MongoDB Atlas

Build a hack using MongoDB Atlas to store and manage its data in the cloud. MLH points entrants to a $50 student credit, an ongoing free tier that requires no credit card, and learning resources from MongoDB University.

**Prize.** An M5Stack IoT Kit for each winning team member.

### Best Domain Name from GoDaddy Registry

Register a domain through the GoDaddy Registry challenge for a chance to win based on the domain you choose for your hack.

**Prize.** Digital gift card. MLH gives no face value on the event page.
